About me

I'm Batuhan Türk, an 18-year-old student at TÜBİTAK Science High School in Istanbul. I have always wanted to become an aeronautical engineer, and this project is where model aircraft, simulation and code came together.

The design, the code, the wind tunnel and the scale model are my own work, carried out at İTO Science and Art Center.
